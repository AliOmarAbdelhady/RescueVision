# Rescue Vision — Brand Identity

> **We watch over a city in its worst hours.**  — slogan (banners, hero, brand guide). Logo lockups use the descriptor *Aerial Rescue Systems* only.

**Aerial Rescue Systems.** The mark is a human iris whose pupil holds the front glass of a camera lens: an eye that sees from above.

## Palette
Navy `#080E1A` · Deep Night `#050810` · Midnight `#0D1626` · Signal Cyan `#00E5FF` · Rescue Amber `#FF8C1A` · Steel `#8EA8C0` · Slate `#5C7A96` · Ink `#1A2538` · Light `#ECEEF2` · White `#FFFFFF`

## Type
Oswald 700 (wordmark, uppercase, -0.015em), Oswald 500 (tagline, 0.46em tracking), JetBrains Mono 500 (tags).

## Usage
Use the supplied files. Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's diameter. Don't recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the mark. Dark theme: white "RESCUE" + steel "VISION". Light theme: ink wordmark on `#ECEEF2`.
