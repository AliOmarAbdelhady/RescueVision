# Limitations and Ethics

## System Limitations

RescueVision is a **research prototype** designed for decision support only. It has the following limitations:

1. **Not a replacement for official assessment.** This system must not be used as the sole basis for emergency response decisions. All outputs require review by qualified emergency management professionals.

2. **Imagery artifacts.** Satellite and drone images may contain clouds, smoke, shadows, resolution artifacts, or georegistration errors that degrade model performance.

3. **Generalization gaps.** Models trained on specific disaster types or geographic regions may not generalize well to unseen locations, disaster types, or building styles.

4. **Label noise.** Damage severity labels in training datasets (xBD, FloodNet) can be subjective, inconsistent, or noisy. This affects model reliability.

5. **Resolution sensitivity.** Performance depends heavily on image resolution. Low-resolution imagery (e.g., >1m/pixel) may produce unreliable building-level assessments.

6. **Temporal gaps.** Pre/post image pairs may be captured days or weeks apart, introducing changes unrelated to the disaster (e.g., construction, seasonal changes).

7. **Class imbalance.** Rare damage classes (e.g., "destroyed") may have lower detection accuracy than common classes ("no-damage").

## Ethical Considerations

1. **Do no harm.** This tool should augment human decision-making, not replace it. False positives (overestimating damage) may waste resources; false negatives (underestimating damage) may endanger lives.

2. **Privacy.** Satellite and drone imagery may capture identifiable information about individuals and properties. Handle data in compliance with local privacy regulations.

3. **Equity.** Model performance may vary across geographic regions and building types. Under-resourced areas with less common building styles may receive less accurate assessments.

4. **Accountability.** Automated assessments should be clearly marked as machine-generated. Human operators must be identifiable and accountable for decisions informed by this system.

5. **Data provenance.** Users should understand the source, date, and limitations of input imagery before trusting model outputs.

## Bias Awareness

- Training data (xBD) primarily covers certain disaster types (hurricanes, earthquakes, floods, wildfires, tsunamis) in specific geographic regions.
- Building types, materials, and urban density vary globally; the model may perform poorly on unfamiliar architectures.
- Damage labeling conventions may not align with local assessment standards.

## Safety Disclaimer

**Every report and output generated by RescueVision includes the following disclaimer:**

> "This report is automatically generated by a research prototype. It is intended for decision support and prioritization only. It must be reviewed by qualified emergency management or remote-sensing professionals before operational use."

## Reporting Issues

If you observe harmful outputs or biases, please document the case and report it as an issue on the project repository.
